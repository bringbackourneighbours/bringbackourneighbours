You can find all our content compiled in the <InternalPathLink path="wizard">Emergency Kit against Deportation</InternalPathLink>. This is a brochure that you can read online or print out.

We also offer <InternalPathLink path="flyer">flyers</InternalPathLink> on specific topics. These flyers are also included in the Emergency Kit. They are designed to be printed out and distributed. They are formatted so that you can print them easily on a regular printer.

If you'd like to put up a poster, we have a [dark blue poster](/poster/bbon-poster-blue-2026.pdf) and a [light-coloured poster](/poster/bbon-poster-white-2026.pdf).

If you'd like to refer people to this page whilst out and about, you can use our [business card](/poster/bbon-businesscard-2026.pdf).

If you'd like to print a larger quantity to distribute, please feel free to email us at info@bringbackourneighbours.de. We can send you materials depending on stock levels. <InternalShortLink identifier="about">We'd also be grateful for any donations</InternalShortLink>.

## All flyers

The flyers are available in two formats:

- as a four-page leaflet in DIN A6
- and as an eight-page "zine" in A7.

### All A6 flyers as leaflets

These print templates contain all the flyers as four-page leaflets.

You should print them double-sided (long edge) on A4 paper. Then you need to cut it horizontally once and fold it vertically once: this will give you two leaflets.

### All flyers as A7 zines

These print templates contain all the flyers as a small mini-zine. [We think zines are really cool!](https://zines.cool/was-ist-ein-zine/)

You should print them single-sided on A4 paper.
A bit of craftwork is needed to turn them into little booklets.

You'll need to fold them a few times and then make a cut:
