Germany is once again cooperating with the Taliban to deport people without residence permits to Afghanistan.

The government says it only deports single men to Afghanistan. But the government has lied about this before.

Nevertheless, many Afghans are living in Germany with temporary leave (Duldung) to remain, and very, very few people are actually deported to Afghanistan.

At present, there is approximately 3 collective deportation to Afghanistan per month. However, deportations on regular passenger flights are also possible.
