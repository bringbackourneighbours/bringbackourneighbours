At present, people are not regularly deported to Syria. In 2025, exactly two people were deported. In 2026, a small number of convicted criminals were also deported to Syria.

There is increasing discussion in German politics about whether more people should return to Syria. It is possible that some people may soon lose their protection in Germany.

However, many people from Syria now have a good chance of staying in Germany permanently with a <InternalShortLink identifier="residence">residence permit</InternalShortLink> or of obtaining German citizenship.
